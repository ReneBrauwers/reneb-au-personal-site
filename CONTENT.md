## Perspective section

**Section eyebrow**

> How I work

**Heading**

> Architecture is useful when it improves the decision.

### Start with the outcome

> Lead with the business problem, the customer or operating consequence, and the decision that needs to be made. Technology comes after context.

### Make trade-offs explicit

> There is rarely one perfect option. Good advice makes the cost, risk, reversibility and organisational impact of each path understandable.

### Stay close to delivery

> Strategy loses value when it cannot survive contact with engineering reality. The feedback loop between intent and execution must stay short.

Optional pull quote:

> Protect the outcome, not the architecture function.

If the quote is used, present it as René's operating principle—not as a third-party testimonial.
