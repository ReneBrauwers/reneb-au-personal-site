## Executive synthesis

René's public footprint shows a credible progression:

1. hands-on software and enterprise integration;
2. deep Microsoft integration and Azure practice;
3. public technical writing, reviewing and community contribution;
4. solution architecture, consulting and delivery leadership; and
5. enterprise architecture leadership focused on technology decisions in regulated financial services.

The strongest personal-brand territory is therefore not “Azure expert” or “enterprise architect” in isolation. It is:

> A business-first technology advisor with builder credibility, using architecture to connect strategy, risk and engineering execution.

The historical sites also reveal a direct, practical voice. Topics repeatedly addressed real implementation problems, security, cost, automation, resilience and professional evolution. That supports a modern page that is crisp and opinionated, but it does not justify reviving an old product-centric identity.

## Copy implications

Use:

- business consequence before technical detail;
- better decisions as the main promise;
- Business, Architecture and Engineering as the organising relationship;
- risk and regulated context as credibility;
- builder-to-advisor progression as the differentiator;
- one understated historical recognition line.

Avoid:

- a long résumé;
- dated product certification lists;
- making Azure the current brand;
- unsupported experience counts;
- logos as credibility substitutes;
- “thought leader” language;
- reproducing the old blog's informal grammar;
- naming clients or confidential outcomes.
